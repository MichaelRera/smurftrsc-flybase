# Privacy policy: SmurfTrsc for FlyBase (browser extension)

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

SmurfTrsc for FlyBase shows gene-expression results from Zane et al. 2023 on FlyBase (flybase.org) report pages. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**What the extension reads.** Only on flybase.org pages: the FlyBase identifier in the page address and, on allele pages, the associated gene and the report's section list. It does not read other websites, form contents or your browsing history.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The dataset ships inside the extension, and it makes no network request of its own.

**What it stores.** One setting in your browser's local extension storage: whether the panel is collapsed.

**No analytics, no advertising, no sale of data.**
